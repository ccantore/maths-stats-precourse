Solution to W04-ST04-PA (S4)

Method. Apply the linear-transformation rules for expectation and variance.

𝔼[Y]=1−2𝔼[X]=1−6=−5. \boxed{ \mathbb{E}[Y] =1-2\mathbb{E}[X] =1-6 =-5.}

The constant shift does not affect dispersion, and the multiplier is squared:

Var⁡(Y)=(−2)2Var⁡(X)=4(2)=8. \boxed{ \operatorname{Var}(Y) =(-2)^2\operatorname{Var}(X) =4(2) =8.}

Adding a constant shifts every realised value by the same amount, so it shifts the expectation but leaves deviations from the expectation unchanged.
