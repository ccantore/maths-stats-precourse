Instructions

Attempt all ten questions and all 17 scorable parts. The target time is 240 minutes and the total is 100 marks. No calculator or software is needed. Give exact values. Write gradients and displacement vectors as columns, put one output component in each Jacobian row, and state dimensions before using the multivariable chain rule.

Questions 9 and 10 form the earlier-outcome retrieval block. Complete them without consulting Weeks 1–3 notes. Use progressive hints only after a serious attempt, and do not open the complete solutions until you have attempted or paused every part.
