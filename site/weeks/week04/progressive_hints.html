Week 4 progressive hints

Use the hints in order. After each hint, return to your own work and continue until you reach a new, specific impasse. The final hint may give a nearly complete setup, but it does not give the final result.

W04-Q01-PA

  1. When finding fxf_x, treat yy, eye^y and yy in the logarithmic term as constants. When finding fyf_y, treat xx as constant.
  2. Put fxf_x above fyf_y in a 2×12\times1 column, then substitute x=1x=1, y=0y=0, e0=1e^0=1 and log⁡1=0\log1=0.

W04-Q01-PB

  1. Differentiate each first partial derivative from Part A with respect to both variables.
  2. Arrange fxx,fxy,fyx,fyyf_{xx},f_{xy},f_{yx},f_{yy} in a 2×22\times2 matrix and then evaluate it.
  3. Ask whether all second partial derivatives are continuous on (0,∞)×ℝ(0,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}; this is the condition relevant to the equality of mixed partials.

  1. The xx-derivative of ez−xe^{z-x} includes the derivative of z−xz-x.
  2. Put the three first partial derivatives in input order x,y,zx,y,z, then evaluate ez−xe^{z-x} at the stated point.

W04-Q06-PB
