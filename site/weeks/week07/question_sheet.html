Problem sheet

Instructions

Attempt all ten questions and all 17 scorable parts. The target time is 240 minutes and the total is 100 marks. No calculator or software is needed. Give exact values unless a question says otherwise.

For every equality-constrained problem, state the constraint encoding before interpreting a multiplier. For every inequality-constrained maximisation problem, use

gj(𝐱)≥0,ℒ=f+∑jλjgj+∑kμkhk,λj≥0. g_j(\mathbf{x})\ge0, \qquad \mathcal{L} =f+\sum_j\lambda_jg_j+\sum_k\mu_kh_k, \qquad \lambda_j\ge0.

Write stationarity, primal feasibility, dual feasibility and complementary slackness. State the parameter space before maximising a likelihood.

Use hints.md only after a serious attempt. Do not consult solutions.md until you have completed or paused every part.

Encode the constraint as 12−x−2y=012-x-2y=0. Form the Lagrangian, solve the first-order and feasibility conditions, and report the candidate (x*,y*)(x^*,y^*) and multiplier μ*\mu^*.

W07-Q01-PB — 7 marks; 14 minutes

Justify why the candidate is the unique global maximiser. Then replace 12 by a resource parameter a>0a>0, derive the value function V(a)V(a), and verify at a=12a=12 that the envelope derivative agrees with the multiplier under the stated encoding. Interpret the sign of the derivative.

Question 5 — two inputs and explicit corner checks (M11; 13 marks; 31 minutes)
