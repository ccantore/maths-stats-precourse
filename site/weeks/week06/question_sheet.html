Instructions

Attempt all 11 questions and all 16 scorable parts. The target time is 270 minutes and the total is 100 marks. Show column gradients, Hessians, distributional assumptions, standardisations and every condition needed for a local, global, exact or approximate conclusion. A basic scientific calculator is permitted only for the final decimal in W06-Q06-PB; the exact answer is sufficient. No software is needed.

Use hints.md only after a serious attempt. Do not consult solutions.md until you have completed or paused every part.

Find every stationary point. Compute the Hessian and use it to classify each stationary point locally.

Question 2 — a strictly concave objective

g(x,y)=8x+6y−x2−y2−xy,(x,y)∈ℝ2. g(x,y)=8x+6y-x^2-y^2-xy, \qquad (x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^2.

Question 4 — when the Hessian test is inconclusive

W06-Q04-PA (8 marks; 22 minutes)

Let

Find the stationary points and evaluate the Hessian there. Explain why the usual definite-Hessian test is inconclusive. Then classify the point by a direct argument, including whether the conclusion is local or global and whether the optimiser is unique. State whether a global maximum exists.
