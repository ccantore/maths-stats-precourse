Week 5 progressive hints

Use the hints in order. After each hint, return to your own work and continue until you reach a new, specific impasse.

W05-Q01-PA

  1. Differentiate with respect to pp and ww separately, keeping the other variable fixed, and place the results in a column.
  2. Use dc=∇c(p,w)𝖳[dp,dw]𝖳\mathrm{d}c=\nabla c(p,w)^{\mathsf T} [\mathrm{d}p,\mathrm{d}w]^{\mathsf T}, then substitute the base point before the stated small changes.

W05-Q01-PB

  1. Regard zz as a function of the three arguments xx, yy and θ\theta. The logarithm requires y(θ)>0y(\theta)>0.
  2. Write z′=zxx′+zyy′+zθz'=z_xx'+z_yy'+z_\theta, retaining the direct partial derivative with respect to θ\theta.
  3. Evaluate x,y,x′x,y,x' and y′y' at zero before combining the three terms.

W05-Q02-PA

  1. The scalar implicit-function condition is Fx≠0F_x\ne0 at the point, in addition to differentiability nearby.
  2. Differentiate F(x(θ),θ)=0F(x(\theta),\theta)=0 to obtain Fxx′+Fθ=0F_xx'+F_\theta=0, then evaluate the two partial derivatives.

W05-Q02-PB

  1. Form the 2×22\times2 Jacobian of (F1,F2)(F_1,F_2) with respect to (x,y)(x,y), not with respect to all three variables.
  2. Check its determinant at the point. Then use 𝐉𝐅,𝐱𝐱′+𝐅θ=𝟎\mathbf{J}_{\mathbf{F},\mathbf{x}}\mathbf{x}' +\mathbf{F}_\theta=\mathbf{0}.
  3. Substitute the point to obtain a 2×22\times2 linear system for x′(0)x'(0) and y′(0)y'(0), and solve that system.

W05-Q03-PA

  1. Replace xx and yy by txtx and tyty, with t>0t>0, and collect the resulting power of tt; then compare xfx+yfyxf_x+yf_y with ff.
  2. For rr, compare r(tx,ty)=tx+t2y2r(tx,ty)=tx+t^2y^2 with a possible single factor tkr(x,y)t^kr(x,y).
  3. At (2,1)(2,1), compute xhx+yhyxh_x+yh_y and compare it with the appropriate multiple of h(x,y)h(x,y), after first scaling hh and calculating its partial derivatives.

W05-Q04-PA

  1. Replace 𝐀\mathbf{A} by (𝐀+𝐀𝖳)/2(\mathbf{A}+\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf T})/2.
  2. Use its eigenvalues, leading principal minors, or a completed-square expression for the quadratic form to determine the sign for every non-zero vector.

W05-Q04-PB

  1. The eigenvectors [1,1]𝖳[1,1]^{\mathsf T} and [1,−1]𝖳[1,-1]^{\mathsf T} diagonalise the matrix for every aa.
  2. Express the two eigenvalues as functions of aa and classify the cases in which both are positive, one is zero, or their signs differ.
  3. Use the positive trace to rule out both eigenvalues being non-positive.

W05-Q05-PA

  1. For CC, take two points and a weight λ∈[0,1]\lambda\in[0,1]. Use the convexity inequality for the square function.
  2. For DD, try two points with the same positive yy-coordinate and opposite xx-coordinates, then inspect their midpoint.

W05-Q05-PB

  1. Compute the Hessian and use its determinant to test whether it is positive or negative semidefinite.
  2. For a level c>0c>0, rewrite the upper contour condition xy≥cxy\ge c as an epigraph condition involving c/xc/x.
  3. Check the curvature of x↦c/xx\mapsto c/x on x>0x>0, and handle levels c≤0c\le0 separately.

W05-Q06-PA

  1. Sum each row to obtain pXp_X and each column to obtain pYp_Y.
  2. Check independence using one joint cell against pX(x)pY(y)p_X(x)p_Y(y); a single failure disproves independence.

W05-Q06-PB

  1. For each fixed yy, divide the two entries in that column by the positive column total.
  2. Because XX takes values zero and one, 𝔼[X∣Y=y]=ℙ(X=1∣Y=y)\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y=y]=\mathbb{P}(X=1\mid Y=y).
  3. Weight the three conditional means by pY(y)p_Y(y) and compare the result with the marginal probability ℙ(X=1)\mathbb{P}(X=1).

W05-Q07-PA

  1. Normalise over the triangular support using ∫01∫0ycdxdy=1\int_0^1\int_0^y c\,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y=1.
  2. For fixed xx, the admissible yy-values run from xx to one. For fixed yy, the admissible xx-values run from zero to yy.
  3. Divide the joint density by fY(y)f_Y(y) and retain both 0<x<y0<x<y and 0<y<10<y<1 in the conditional support.

W05-Q07-PB

  1. Integrate xfX∣Y(x∣y)x f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y) over the conditional support.
  2. Use 𝔼[X]=𝔼[𝔼[X∣Y]]\mathbb{E}[X]=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]] with the marginal density of YY.
  3. For the direct check, integrate xfX,Y(x,y)x f_{X,Y}(x,y) over the original triangle in the same order used for normalisation.

W05-Q08-PA

  1. Write the two conditional means and variances of the Bernoulli distribution as functions of YY.
  2. Calculate 𝔼[Var⁡(X∣Y)]\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)] and Var⁡(𝔼[X∣Y])\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]) separately before adding them.
  3. For the verification, use total probability to find ℙ(X=1)\mathbb{P}(X=1), then apply the Bernoulli variance formula.

W05-Q09-PA

  1. Form the characteristic polynomial det⁡(𝐊−λ𝐈2)\det(\mathbf{K}-\lambda\mathbf{I}_2).
  2. Solve one homogeneous system for each eigenvalue. Two independent eigenvectors in ℝ2\mathbb{R}^2 settle diagonalizability; place them in a matrix and use the corresponding eigenvalues on the diagonal.

W05-Q10-PA

  1. Treat A,AcA,A^c as a partition and use the law of total probability for CC.
  2. Bayes’ rule divides ℙ(C∣A)ℙ(A)\mathbb{P}(C\mid A)\mathbb{P}(A) by the total probability just found.

W05-Q10-PB

  1. Use linearity of expectation on 4−2U4-2U.
  2. For variance, the additive constant disappears and the coefficient is squared.
