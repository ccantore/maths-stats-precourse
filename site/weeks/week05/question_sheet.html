Instructions

Attempt all ten questions and all 17 scorable parts. The target time is 240 minutes and the total is 100 marks. No calculator or software is needed. Show every regularity, nonsingularity, domain or support condition needed for your method. Gradients and vectors are columns. Give exact values.

Use hints.md only after a serious attempt. Do not consult solutions.md until you have completed or paused every part.
