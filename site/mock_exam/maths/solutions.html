Question 1

Part A — MOCK-M0-Q01-PA (M1)

Method and conditions. Negate the universal quantifier and the inequality, then exhibit one real number for which the negated inequality holds.

The negation is

For example, x=1/2x=1/2 gives x2=1/4<1/2=xx^2=1/4<1/2=x.

Final result. The displayed existential statement is the negation, and x=1/2x=1/2 is a counterexample.
