Two-paper mock examination

The mock consists of two independent papers:

  • Maths: candidate paper, formula sheet and solutions. MOCK_MATHS allows 90 minutes for 55 marks and covers M1–M11.
  • Probability and Statistics: candidate paper, formula sheet, and solutions. MOCK_STATS allows 90 minutes for 45 marks and covers S1–S9.

Attempt each candidate paper before opening its solutions.

For this controlled first-cohort pilot, mock scores are diagnostic only. They are not used for admission, progression, ranking, funding or grading, and the materials remain subject to the applicable human academic and release gates.
